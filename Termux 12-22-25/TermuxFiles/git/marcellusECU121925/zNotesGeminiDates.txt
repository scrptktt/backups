Other Significant Celebrations
Sabbath: A weekly day of rest and worship.
Purim: Commemorating deliverance from Haman (Esther).
Temple Dedication: A week-long celebration led by Solomon with feasting and music (1 Kings 8).
Wedding at Cana: Jesus' first miracle, highlighting joy in celebration (John 2).
Early Church Gatherings: Believers gathered with gladness, praise, and fellowship (Acts 2:46-47). 
New Testament & Christian Celebrations
Lord's Supper (Communion): A remembrance of Christ's sacrifice.
Baptism: A symbolic new beginning.
Christian Calendar: Includes Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, etc., focused on Christ's life and salvation. 
Themes of Celebration in the Bible
Remembrance: Remembering God's past acts of deliverance and faithfulness (Passover).
Joy & Strength: Finding strength in God's presence (Nehemiah 8:10).
Worship & Praise: Expressing honor through music, dancing, and feasting.
Spiritual Discipline: A commanded part of a life lived for God, not just a dry existence.

US holidays celebrate American history, values, and culture, from federal days like Independence Day (July 4th) for freedom and Thanksgiving (Nov.) for harvest/gratitude, to cultural days like Halloween (Oct. 31st) for fun, honoring figures like MLK Jr. (Jan.), and commemorating events like Memorial Day (May) for fallen soldiers, with many focusing on national unity, workers' rights, and remembrance.
